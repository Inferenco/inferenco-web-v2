# Nemesis Go Privacy Policy

This policy takes effect when published and remains in effect until updated.

This policy covers the Nemesis Go app for Android and iOS and any correspondence about the app. Singularity Shift Ltd is the Android publisher and Spielcrypto LLC is the iOS publisher. Each company is responsible for the personal information it handles in connection with its version of the app.

## Gameplay and information on your device

You can play Nemesis Go offline and without an app account. The computer opponent and the analysis behind move suggestions run on your device. The app stores your saved game, recent results, preferences and tutorial progress on your device so you can resume play and keep your settings. It also stores information used to space out ads, check the local game model and remember whether your Remove ads subscription was active when it last checked. We do not receive your moves or saved games through the app.

## Ads and privacy choices

When advertising is enabled, Nemesis Go uses Google AdMob to serve ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to handle ad privacy choices. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device or advertising identifiers where available, interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic information. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, manage how often ads appear, and detect fraud or abuse. The information used and the ads you see can vary with your region, device settings and privacy choices. [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) · [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Where applicable, Google presents a privacy message to let you make choices about advertising. Android provides advertising controls in device settings. On iOS, you can manage whether Nemesis Go has permission to track in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**. You can also manage ad personalisation for your Google account through Google's controls. [Google My Ad Center](https://support.google.com/My-Ad-Center-Help/answer/12155451?co=GENIE.Platform&hl=en)

## Purchases

If you buy or restore a Remove ads subscription, Google Play on Android or the App Store on iOS handles the transaction and processes your payment information. Nemesis Go receives store-provided information such as the subscription product, transaction reference and subscription status to confirm or restore access. Payment details stay with the store or its payment providers. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Support and legal reasons for use

If you email us about Nemesis Go, we use your email address, your message and anything you choose to send to respond and keep track of the request. Our email provider handles that correspondence as part of delivering the service. We may disclose information when required by law.

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we rely on your consent for advertising activities that require it, on performance of our agreement with you to provide purchased access, on our legitimate interests in responding to support requests and helping prevent abuse, and on legal obligations when they apply. Google and Apple may process data outside your country. Their privacy policies describe where they process information and the safeguards they use for international transfers. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Storage, retention and deletion

Gameplay records and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They stay there until the app replaces them or that storage is cleared. On Android, you can clear the app's storage in device settings. On iOS, choose **Delete App** to remove its local data. If your device makes backups, copies may remain according to your backup settings. Google and Apple handle retention of their own ad and store records under their policies.

We keep support emails for as long as needed to respond, handle relevant follow-up or meet an applicable record-keeping obligation. You can contact the publisher for your platform to request deletion of a specific support conversation.

To ask about this policy or exercise your rights, contact the publisher for your platform.

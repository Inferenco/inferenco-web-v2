# Inferenco Website Privacy Policy

This notice explains how the Inferenco team handles information when you visit [inferenco.com](/) or contact us about the website. Inferenco is a collaboration between Singularity Shift and Spielcrypto.

## Information used to run the website

- **Hosting.** GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security. See [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Website statistics.** We use Overtracking to understand how people use the site and improve it. The analytics service can receive information about visits, such as the pages requested, the referring site and browser or device information. See the [Overtracking data processing agreement](https://overtracking.com/dpa) for information about its service.
- **Display preferences.** The website stores your chosen theme and language in your browser. These preferences let the site remember your choices on later visits. You can clear them through your browser’s site-data settings.
- **Page resources.** Your browser may contact other services to load icons, blog posts and their media. Those service providers receive the information your browser sends with the request, including its IP address.

## When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address, your message and any attachments you send to respond and follow up. Our email providers handle that correspondence. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry, handle relevant follow-up or meet a record-keeping obligation that applies.

## Apps, games and external sites

Each app or game has its own privacy information. For example, see the [Nemesis Go Privacy Policy](/nemesis-go/privacy/). If you follow a link to an app store, social platform or another website, that service handles your visit under its own privacy notice.

## Questions and privacy requests

Email [singularityshift@inferenco.com](mailto:singularityshift@inferenco.com) or [spielcrypto@inferenco.com](mailto:spielcrypto@inferenco.com) with website privacy questions or requests. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict or object to its use, or complain to your data protection authority. In the UK, you can contact the [Information Commissioner’s Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

If we update this notice, we will publish the new version here.

# Support

Need help with one of our games or apps? Contact the publisher for your platform. We reply as soon as we can.

## Android — Singularity Shift Ltd.

For our Android games and apps, email [singularityshift@inferenco.com](mailto:singularityshift@inferenco.com).

## iOS — Spielcrypto LLC.

For our iOS games and apps, email [spielcrypto@inferenco.com](mailto:spielcrypto@inferenco.com).

## What to include

Tell us the app name, your device and operating-system version, and what happened. Screenshots or a short description of the steps help us reproduce the issue.
